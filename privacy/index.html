Daily Bible: Illustrated

Privacy Policy

Effective 19 November 2026

This policy explains what the Daily Bible: Illustrated app (“the app”) does with information about you. The app is published by Kazka (“we”, “us”). In short: there is no account and no sign-in. We show no ads and do not track you. Your reading stays on your device.

1. Information that stays on your device

The app keeps the following on your iPhone only. We do not receive it:

  • Your reading progress, streaks and goals.
  • Your settings, such as translation, text size and reminder time.
  • Stories, images and audio you have downloaded.

Deleting the app removes this information. Daily reminders are set up on your device with Apple’s local notifications. We do not run a push notification server.

2. Downloading content

The app downloads stories, illustrations and narration from our content server, which our hosting provider (Supabase) runs. Like any internet request, this one shows the server your IP address and basic technical details, such as the time and the file requested. The hosting provider may keep these details in short-term logs for security and to keep the service working. We do not use them to identify you or to build a profile of you.

3. Problem reports you choose to send

If you use “Report a problem”, we receive:

  • The type of problem and the message you write.
  • The verse, scene and translation you were reading.
  • The app version.
  • A random ID created when the app is installed. It is not linked to your name, Apple Account or device serial number.
  • A salted, one-way hash of your IP address, which we use only to limit spam and abuse.

We use reports to fix text, images and pronunciation. Please do not put personal information in your message. We keep reports for as long as we need them to fix the problem and improve the content.

4. Purchases

Apple handles Membership payments through your Apple Account. We never see your payment details, name or email address. The app asks Apple on your device whether you have an active subscription. In App Store Connect, Apple gives us sales reports that do not identify you.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not use advertising or tracking SDKs, and we do not share or sell data to data brokers or advertisers.
  • We do not use third-party analytics. If we add privacy-friendly analytics later, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy label first.
  • We do not send anything about you to AI providers. Illustrations and narration are made in advance, using AI tools and human review. None of them are made from your information.

6. Service providers

  • Supabase: hosts the content server and receives problem reports.
  • Apple: App Store distribution, payments and notifications. Apple’s own privacy policy applies.

These providers may process information in countries other than yours. Where the law requires it, the transfer is covered by appropriate safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses.

7. Legal basis (EEA and UK)

We process content requests and problem reports because of our legitimate interest in delivering and improving the app and protecting it from abuse. When you send a report, we also rely on your choice to send it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete information about you, to object to its processing, or to complain to your data protection authority. We hold no account and no name or email, so in most cases we cannot link information to you. If you contact us, we will help where we can, for example by deleting a report you describe to us.

9. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. It asks no one for personal information, including children.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the new version here and update the date at the top. Significant changes will also be noted in the app’s release notes.
